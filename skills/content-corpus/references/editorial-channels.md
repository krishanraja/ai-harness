## Shared distinction

**Canon, 2026-08-28, restated for the OS 2026-08-29.** The authority for this file is `github.com/krishanraja/mindmake`, `project-documentation/02_PUBLICATION.md`. There is one publication and it runs **exactly two channels: The Money of AI and Built with AI. There is no third.** Do not invent one, do not blend them, do not revive an earlier name for either. Techonomic and The Builder Economy are retired as brands; their useful registers survive in The Money of AI and Built with AI respectively. Signal & Noise is a distribution and discussion channel, not a channel of the publication. Make Your Mind Up is the lead magnet and URL into CTRL at `makeyourmindup.ai`, not a content brand.

**Long-form is the asset. Social is the trailer.** LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and podcast appearances are all distribution for these two, never formats of their own.

**The five standards. Every asset must be all five, and failing any one blocks publication.**

1. **Unique**, built on a real owned artifact rather than a summary of the news.
2. **Researched**, with every material number, quote and event sourced.
3. **Thoughtful**, naming the mechanism and steelmanning the best counterpoint.
4. **Kind**, warm with people and sharp with ideas.
5. **Helpful**, leaving the viewer a usable decision rule, frame or next move.

If a piece has no owned artifact, flag the gap rather than faking uniqueness.

These are purpose and format playbooks. They do not prove that a format or channel is active, reachable, publishing on a stated cadence, or still using the same hosts. Run the status gate in `SKILL.md` before production.

Editorial work should demonstrate a point of view and usefulness rather than smuggle in an offer CTA. Use a verified, current operator artifact wherever possible.

## Mindmake's publication (the publication and house register)

**Durable role:** the content arm of Mindmake, the advisory. The branded destination is `live.themindmaker.ai`. Its intended commercial model includes paid subscriptions, and it is top of funnel for both Mindmake doors: the managed advisory and CTRL. Retrieve the live site before claiming current tiers, access, price, cadence, or destination behavior; do not substitute an underlying vendor URL without approval.

**Register for both formats:** tongue in cheek, dark humour, dry rather than zany. The comedy comes from the gap between what is claimed and what the evidence says, so the funniest line is often the accurate one placed next to the claim.

Two rules stop that register from eating itself:

- **The Kind Rule.** Irony points at claims, hype, incentives, and decisions, never at a named person's competence or character. "This pricing page assumes nobody owns a calculator" is in register. "This CEO is an idiot" is not.
- **The Rigour Rule.** The register may be sarcastic; the evidence handling is humourless. **The joke can never be the finding.** Attribute every load-bearing number to the party that produced it. Where the record runs out, say so plainly and without a punchline.

## The Money of AI

**Mandate:** investigate how the digital world gets paid for as attention, content, distribution, access, and the open web change. The name is the question the channel always asks. This is the useful editorial register carried over from Techonomic.

- Audience: operators, founders, publishers, media commercial leaders, and strategically curious builders.
- Stance: interrogative and investigative, with a committed view earned through the inquiry.
- Characteristic question: who pays for this, where does value move, and what mechanism changes when the shift lands?
- Native form: durable essay, normally long enough to trace a mechanism and a counterargument. Confirm the current publishing format live.
- Opening: a structural framing, historical inversion, or economic tension; never generic AI trend context.
- Closing: a forward-looking verdict or reusable economic lens; never "time will tell."
- Strongest artifact: a commercial pattern, experiment, or P&L reality Krish has observed directly.
- Antagonist: incentives or a failing model, not a named villain.
- Helpful test: the reader can apply the money question to their own corner of the digital economy.

**The beat is narrow.** In scope: pricing and packaging, unit economics, positioning and competitive strategy, build/buy/partner, human labour, and who captures value. Out of scope by default: model releases, benchmarks, capability announcements, governance or regulation commentary, pilot or partnership announcements, and funding rounds as events. A model release enters only when the piece is about what it does to somebody's margin. **The event is never the story.**

Krish's adtech and identity background may appear as evidence where relevant, and that history is deliberately kept live. It must never shrink the subject into a disguised product argument, and a retired brand is never offered as a current venture.

## Built with AI

**Mandate:** explore the people using AI to move from creator or operator to builder, and reach the human reason underneath the stated reason for the build. The durable thesis is that, just as the creator economy meant anyone could broadcast, the builder economy means anyone can build with AI.

- Audience: builders, would-be builders, creators crossing into building, and people looking for permission plus a usable first step.
- Stance: energetic, warm, scrappy, generous, and capable of dry humour.
- Characteristic move: surface why, then strategic why, then human why. Ask warmly; the guest should feel seen, not interrogated.
- Native form: builder-to-builder conversation, written up, with cutdowns built around the most retellable human or practical moment.
- Opening: enter through the build, origin scene, or strange decision, not the guest's CV.
- Closing: momentum and an accessible handhold that makes the reader feel able to begin.
- Strongest artifact: the guest's lived story, met by Krish's own builder experience.
- Antagonist: the gate that says building requires permission, credentials, or a large team.
- Helpful test: inspiration is paired with how the first imperfect version actually happened.

The house sarcasm dials down here. Aim irony at the industry around the builder, never at the builder. The guest must finish looking more human, not more foolish. Do not turn the conversation into a stack tour: the third turn of the why is worth more than a list of tools.

## Signal & Noise (a channel, not a format)

Do not commission a new format "for" Signal & Noise. Make an investigation for The Money of AI or a Built with AI conversation first, then adapt it as a live argument for this distribution channel rather than reading an essay aloud.

At the confirmed refocus, Signal & Noise was a co-hosted podcast feed with Rio Longacre and Brett House. Its feed, GUID, subscribers, and public positioning were deliberately left untouched. Verify current hosts, feed, access, and format before operational work, and do not announce a repositioning without exact approval.

- The adversarial, devil's-advocate register belongs to the room, not to the research. The finding still comes from the source format's evidence bar.
- Stance in the room: combative toward ideas, warm toward people. Enter mid-argument, never with a ceremonial welcome. Close on clarity about the disagreement rather than a bow.
- The hard kindness check: devil's-advocate the idea, never sneer at the people who hold it. A what-if that is only edgy is noise.

## Repurposing without duplication

A single source may become multiple assets only when each earns a distinct treatment:

- **The Money of AI** traces the economic mechanism and asks who pays.
- **Built with AI** finds the human motive and the usable beginning.

**The routing test:** if the interesting thing is a flow of money, it is The Money of AI. If the interesting thing is a person's decision to make something, it is Built with AI. If it is genuinely both, produce two assets with two different spines, never one hybrid.

## Named formats. Do not invent formats.

**The Money of AI:** Money Trace (6 to 10 min, one mechanism end to end, built around a diagram that grows on screen). The Artifact (3 to 5 min, one live public artifact interrogated on screen; timestamp and archive it before recording). Verdict (90 sec to 3 min, a committed call with the counterargument stated and answered). Cold-open cutdown (45 to 90 sec, vertical, must work muted so the number is on screen as text).

**The investigative format is called The Artifact, never Teardown**, because The Teardown is a retired Mindmake offer and reusing the word would revive a name the canon retires.

**Built with AI:** Builder conversation (25 to 45 min, the primary asset, everything else is cut from it). The Build Itself (3 to 6 min, screen recording of the actual thing including its rough edges, never a clean re-record). The Third Why (60 to 120 sec, the most retellable human moment, selected for emotional truth not the smartest sentence). First Version (45 to 90 sec, vertical, ends on the handhold not the outcome).

**Two supply risks to design around.** The Money of AI is a mechanism channel and mechanisms do not survive a man talking in a room: without a visual scaffold, keep the piece written and produce only Verdict and cutdown. Built with AI needs guests and booking is the bottleneck, so keep a solo variant running, held to the same three-why standard on Krish himself, so the pipeline does not stall when the calendar is empty.

## Automatic rejection

Reject and return for rework if any of these are true.

- The spine is an announcement, funding round, model release or benchmark, and no margin consequence is traced. (The Money of AI)
- The cut is predominantly a list of tools. (Built with AI)
- A load-bearing number appears without an attributed source.
- Irony lands on a named person rather than on a claim, incentive or decision.
- The piece has no owned artifact and restates publicly available commentary.
- The close is a summary rather than a verdict, lens or handhold.
- A cutdown reuses paragraphs from another surface without a distinct angle.
- The asset asserts cadence, subscriber numbers, pricing, host lineup or channel status that has not been verified against the live publication.
- Anything was published, scheduled or sent without per-asset approval.

## Banned language

In scripts, titles and captions: "AI transformation", "AI literacy", "innovation", "capability building", "leverage AI", "in today's fast-moving landscape", "time will tell", "the future of work", "game-changer", "unlock". Each is an abstraction standing in for a commercial claim. Replace it with the specific mechanism, number or decision it was hiding.

No em dashes anywhere, including on-screen text and code.

## Titles

- **The Money of AI** titles ask or answer a money question. "Who actually pays for AI search" works. "The state of AI search in 2026" does not.
- **Built with AI** titles enter through the build or the decision. "He rebuilt his agency's entire delivery model in a weekend" works. "Interview with [name], founder of [company]" does not.

## Stop at the authority boundary

Deliver drafts and rendered files. Publish, schedule or post nothing without explicit per-asset approval from Krish Raja.
- The **Signal & Noise** room stress-tests whichever finished format is airing.
- Social cutdowns such as LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube ride the source format's register; they do not become a third commissioned format.

Copy-pasting paragraphs across surfaces fails even if openings or lengths differ. Record the shared source, then create a distinct angle, structure, evidence emphasis, and audience payoff. Long-form is the asset; social is the trailer.
