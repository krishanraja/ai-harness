## Shared distinction

**Canon, 2026-09-19. Supersedes the two-channel rule of 2026-08-28.** The authority for what a subchannel IS is `venture_formats.mandate` in Mindmaker OS, read live, never a file. This document names the subchannels and the boundaries between them; it does not restate a mandate, because a restated mandate drifts while the table does not. That drift is exactly what happened to the rule this paragraph replaces: it said "exactly two channels, there is no third", and eleven hardcoded lists across four repositories still encode it.

**The publication is `makeyourmindup`, and it runs three subchannels:** `split_the_bill` (Wednesdays), `mind_the_gap` (Fridays), `lift_the_lid` (standing, no fixed day). Do not invent a fourth and do not blend them. **The Money of AI and Built with AI are retired**, along with Techonomic, The Builder Economy, inspect.the.build, follow.the.money and Newsflash. Signal & Noise is a distribution and discussion channel, not a subchannel of the publication. **Make Your Mind Up is the content brand and owns the name**; it was previously the lead magnet into CTRL, and CTRL moves to its own address at a naming gate that is still open.

**Long-form is the asset. Social is the trailer.** LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and podcast appearances are all distribution for the three, never subchannels of their own.

**The five standards. Every asset must be all five, and failing any one blocks publication.**

1. **Unique**, built on a real owned artifact rather than a summary of the news.
2. **Researched**, with every material number, quote and event sourced.
3. **Thoughtful**, naming the mechanism and steelmanning the best counterpoint.
4. **Kind**, warm with people and sharp with ideas.
5. **Helpful**, leaving the viewer a usable decision rule, frame or next move.

If a piece has no owned artifact, flag the gap rather than faking uniqueness.

These are purpose and format playbooks. They do not prove that a format or channel is active, reachable, publishing on a stated cadence, or still using the same hosts. Run the status gate in `SKILL.md` before production.

Editorial work should demonstrate a point of view and usefulness rather than smuggle in an offer CTA. Use a verified, current operator artifact wherever possible.

## Mindmake's publication (the publication and house register)

**Durable role:** the content arm of Mindmake, the advisory. The branded destination is open: the recommendation is the cover on `makeyourmindup.ai` and the publication on `read.makeyourmindup.ai`, because a Substack custom domain takes the whole host and the two cannot share one. `live.themindmaker.ai` is the retired address. Its intended commercial model includes paid subscriptions, and it is top of funnel for both Mindmake doors: the managed advisory and CTRL. Retrieve the live site before claiming current tiers, access, price, cadence, or destination behavior; do not substitute an underlying vendor URL without approval.

**Register for both formats:** tongue in cheek, dark humour, dry rather than zany. The comedy comes from the gap between what is claimed and what the evidence says, so the funniest line is often the accurate one placed next to the claim.

Two rules stop that register from eating itself:

- **The Kind Rule.** Irony points at claims, hype, incentives, and decisions, never at a named person's competence or character. "This pricing page assumes nobody owns a calculator" is in register. "This CEO is an idiot" is not.
- **The Rigour Rule.** The register may be sarcastic; the evidence handling is humourless. **The joke can never be the finding.** Attribute every load-bearing number to the party that produced it. Where the record runs out, say so plainly and without a punchline.

## The three subchannels

**The mandate for each one lives in `venture_formats.mandate` and is read live.** What
follows is the shape and the boundary, which is what a router needs and what a mandate
does not carry. Anything below that contradicts the table is wrong and the table wins.

| slug | the standing question | diagram |
|---|---|---|
| `split_the_bill` | What does it really cost to run, and who ends up holding the bill? | the money map |
| `mind_the_gap` | What is actually happening, against what everyone says is happening? | the timeline |
| `lift_the_lid` | Does this make its user sharper, or dependent? | the annotated product shot |

**`split_the_bill`** runs Wednesdays. The reader is the buyer with a bill landing. Media,
publishing and advertising monetisation are out of scope: the channel does not go where
Krish is already comfortable.

**`mind_the_gap`** runs Fridays and is the hero. It traces the gap through **one topic per
piece**: the topic is the spine and the gap is the argument. A piece that surveys several
topics is not this format.

**`lift_the_lid`** is standing with no fixed day, publishing when a subject earns it,
because a third fixed slot costs hours the two-to-four-hour rule does not have. **Product
surfaces are its own territory**, not evidence borrowed from another: pricing pages,
default settings, changelogs, free tiers, release notes, and the gap between a keynote and
what a normal account can reach on day one.

### The boundary, which decides every contested subject

`split_the_bill` and `lift_the_lid` can both be handed the same pricing page. **The
question decides, never the surface.** If the piece asks what it costs and who ends up
holding the bill, it is `split_the_bill`. If it asks whether the thing makes its user
sharper or dependent, it is `lift_the_lid`. This boundary is written into both mandates in
the table, so a router reads it from the same place a writer does.

If a subject genuinely reads as two subchannels, that is not a blend. Produce two pieces
with two different spines, or say which question is the real one and drop the other.

### The house register, which is the same in all three

Tongue in cheek, dark humour, dry rather than zany. The comedy comes from the gap between
what is claimed and what the evidence says, so the funniest line is often the accurate one
placed next to the claim. Two rules stop that eating itself:

- **The Kind Rule.** Irony points at claims, hype, incentives and decisions, never at a
  named person's competence or character.
- **The Rigour Rule.** The register may be sarcastic; the evidence handling is humourless.
  **The joke can never be the finding.** Attribute every load-bearing number to the party
  that produced it. Where the record runs out, say so plainly and without a punchline.

**No preaching.** No closing moral, no lesson for leaders, no sentence telling the reader
what to conclude. This overrides every other instruction here and in any mandate.

## Signal & Noise (a channel, not a format)

Do not commission a new format "for" Signal & Noise. Make a piece for one of the three subchannels first, then adapt it as a live argument for this distribution channel rather than reading an essay aloud.

At the confirmed refocus, Signal & Noise was a co-hosted podcast feed with Rio Longacre and Brett House. Its feed, GUID, subscribers, and public positioning were deliberately left untouched. Verify current hosts, feed, access, and format before operational work, and do not announce a repositioning without exact approval.

- The adversarial, devil's-advocate register belongs to the room, not to the research. The finding still comes from the source format's evidence bar.
- Stance in the room: combative toward ideas, warm toward people. Enter mid-argument, never with a ceremonial welcome. Close on clarity about the disagreement rather than a bow.
- The hard kindness check: devil's-advocate the idea, never sneer at the people who hold it. A what-if that is only edgy is noise.

## Repurposing without duplication

A single source may become multiple assets only when each earns a distinct treatment:

- **`split_the_bill`** traces what it costs to run and who ends up holding the bill.
- **`mind_the_gap`** traces one topic over time and shows where the claim and the reality separated.
- **`lift_the_lid`** takes the product apart and asks whether it makes its user sharper or dependent.

**The routing test is a question, never a surface.** The same pricing page can belong to
`split_the_bill` or to `lift_the_lid`. Ask what the piece is really asking: what it costs
and who pays, or sharper or dependent. If a subject genuinely reads as two, produce two
pieces with two different spines, never one hybrid. If it reads as none of the three, it is
not a piece for this publication and saying so is the correct answer.

## Named formats. Do not invent formats.

**Every signature piece is the same package**, whichever subchannel it belongs to: the
written piece at 1,500 to 2,500 words, the opening `your.call` artifact, one diagram in the
subchannel's own language, the audio version, and the vertical cut published the same day.
The vertical cut must work muted, with any number on screen as text.

**The investigative format is called The Artifact, never Teardown**, because The Teardown is a retired Mindmake offer and reusing the word would revive a name the canon retires.

**A separate vocabulary exists and its status is open.** `Money Trace`, `The Artifact`,
`The Third Why` and `First Version` are studio output formats in the production engine AND
are named as retired publication formats in the fleet brief. `The Artifact` was itself the
2026-08-29 rename away from a retired name. Two vocabularies collide on those names, no
tool should pick a winner, and the ruling is Krish's. See
`makeyourmindup:project-documentation/02_REPO_BRIEF.md`.

**Two supply risks to design around.** A mechanism does not survive a man talking in a
room: without a visual scaffold, keep the piece written and produce only the verdict and
the cutdown. And anything needing a guest has booking as its bottleneck, so keep a solo
variant running so the pipeline does not stall when the calendar is empty.

## Automatic rejection

Reject and return for rework if any of these are true.

- The spine is an announcement, funding round, model release or benchmark, and no consequence is traced. (`split_the_bill`)
- The cut is predominantly a list of tools. (`lift_the_lid`)
- A load-bearing number appears without an attributed source.
- Irony lands on a named person rather than on a claim, incentive or decision.
- The piece has no owned artifact and restates publicly available commentary.
- The close is a summary rather than a verdict, lens or handhold.
- A cutdown reuses paragraphs from another surface without a distinct angle.
- The asset asserts cadence, subscriber numbers, pricing, host lineup or channel status that has not been verified against the live publication.
- Anything was published, scheduled or sent without per-asset approval.

## Banned language

In scripts, titles and captions: "AI transformation", "AI literacy", "innovation", "capability building", "leverage AI", "in today's fast-moving landscape", "time will tell", "the future of work", "game-changer", "unlock". Each is an abstraction standing in for a commercial claim. Replace it with the specific mechanism, number or decision it was hiding.

No em dashes anywhere, including on-screen text and code.

## Titles

- **`split_the_bill`** titles ask or answer a money question. "Who actually pays for AI search" works. "The state of AI search in 2026" does not.
- **`lift_the_lid`** titles name the product and what was opened. "Gemini Enterprise, inspected: what exactly is a seat now?" works. "The state of enterprise AI in 2026" does not.
- **`mind_the_gap`** titles name the topic and imply the gap. "The calculator kids turned out fine" works. "Thoughts on AI and education" does not.

## Stop at the authority boundary

Deliver drafts and rendered files. Publish, schedule or post nothing without explicit per-asset approval from Krish Raja.
- The **Signal & Noise** room stress-tests whichever finished format is airing.
- Social cutdowns such as LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube ride the source format's register; they do not become a third commissioned format.

Copy-pasting paragraphs across surfaces fails even if openings or lengths differ. Record the shared source, then create a distinct angle, structure, evidence emphasis, and audience payoff. Long-form is the asset; social is the trailer.
