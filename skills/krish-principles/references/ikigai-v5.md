# Krish Raja: Master Ikigai, version 5 (verbatim)

This is the full ikigai Krish built from 144 ranked answers, exported verbatim on
2026-11-24 from his spreadsheet `Krish_Master_Ikigai_2026_v5` (9 sheets, version 5 of
12 September 2026). Every cell is reproduced in order. It supersedes version 4, which
stays in `ikigai-v4.md` as history (Krish, 2026-11-24: "v5 Ikigai is the most recent").
Krish chose to publish his ikigai here (Ruling, Krish, 2026-10-05). The file id and
owning account are withheld as infrastructure identifiers. No private individual other
than Krish is named in the sheet; company names are kept.

How to read it:

- The spreadsheet is the source. If this copy and the sheet differ, the sheet wins and
  this file is re-exported, never edited by hand.
- Lines that begin with `> Harness annotation` were added by the harness. They are not
  part of the sheet and they never change a cell.
- **Ongoing.** The twelve month commitment of section 5 continues by Krish's ruling of
  2026-10-06 ("its ongoing."), which came after this version, and no new stop date has
  been set. The dates in sections 5, 6, 7 and 9 are history: do not act on the plan's
  steps, the scorecard targets or the stop rule as if they were live, and do not invent a
  new stop date.
- **Tabs version 5 no longer has.** Sections 6 and 8 still point to a CTRL tab and a
  Control Center tab. Version 5 dropped those two tabs and Mindmake Positioning; their
  last copies are in `ikigai-v4.md`.
- **Layout.** Each sheet is a `## Sheet:` heading, and each run of rows between blank
  rows is a table with no separator line. A one-cell row is a title. Rows shorter than
  their table are padded with empty cells, and a `|` inside a cell is written `\|`.
- Appendix D is the version 3 reading and its seven rules. Section 4 of the Master Ikigai
  sheet ("Decision rules v2") supersedes them; Appendix D is history.
