## Make Your Mind Up

Repositories: krishanraja/content-engine, krishanraja/content-watcher, krishanraja/growth-makeyourmindup, krishanraja/makeyourmindup.

- [voice (tone), approved 2026-11-04, claim 03f25f8f-2f4d-4ca0-9cd1-21eb101cdb3c] He rejects any line that doesn't make clear sense when read aloud, including vague pronouns like 'it' or 'the company' that leave the listener unsure what's being referenced. So: Before a line ships, read it aloud and replace any pronoun or vague referent with the actual named thing.
- [voice (packaging), approved 2026-11-04, claim 4b77c851-1226-4d72-8f5d-5dffe726d4f0] He rejects insider shorthand or jargon that only makes sense to someone who already has context, insisting every audience-facing line name the real thing in plain words. So: Replace coded terms, internal callbacks, or unexplained shorthand with plain, nameable language before anything reaches an audience.
- [voice (opening), approved 2026-11-04, claim 944e12dc-646b-46c4-a8bb-ee3fb907523a] He prefers titles and headings that hold back the key twist or finding as a question or surprising reveal, rather than stating the answer outright. So: Write titles as an open question or a withheld twist rather than announcing the conclusion.
- [voice (packaging), approved 2026-11-04, claim b0b7764e-ecfb-4f62-a25b-1c1f7d33cfcf] He approves a title only after seeing it alongside the finished video, in one packaging pass, not before. So: Never ask for title approval before the cut is locked; present title options paired with the finished video for a single sign-off pass.
