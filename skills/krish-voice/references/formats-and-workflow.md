# Krish Voice Formats and Workflow

Load the section that matches the requested artifact after its facts, argument, audience, and authority boundaries are locked.

## Extract the real angle

Do not jump from raw material to prose. If angle selection is still open, hand it to the relevant domain/channel owner and `krish-content-marketer`; this reference helps express an approved angle, not seize strategy ownership.

For an approved argument, confirm:

- the conventional framing it rejects;
- what Krish's authorised experience or the evidence shows instead;
- the fair antagonist and strongest countercase;
- the operator artifact or primary evidence that earns the claim;
- the audience consequence and commercial mechanism;
- the one-sentence claim, written so a reasonable person could disagree.

If the claim remains a topic, contains weasel words, or could be written without Krish's evidence, return it for strategy correction before drafting.

## Choose the container

- **Inversion:** state the conventional take, show its failure, and build to the real insight.
- **Mechanism:** name what causes an observed outcome, explain it, then show the commercial consequence.
- **Inflection point:** establish what changed and what that changes for named actors.
- **Practitioner's confession:** one thing Krish got wrong, why the mistake was convenient, and what corrected it.
- **Forced choice:** expose false optionality and make the real choice visible.
- **Longitudinal take:** trace how a formerly true assumption stopped being true.
- **I built this and here's what broke:** show the artifact, expected behaviour, observed failure, mechanism, and implication.

Use the smallest structure that carries the argument. Do not default to intro, body, conclusion.

## Long-form and social prose

### Opening

Begin inside the argument with the sharpest supported claim, a concrete scene, or a surprising fact. Context comes second. Never use the one-line hook, blank line, explanation template.

### Body

Move in one-to-three-sentence bursts with deliberate variance. Every paragraph advances the argument. Use named companies, real numbers, and actual events only when the evidence packet supports their scope. Include one honest countercase, then commit.

### Ending

End on the hardest forward-looking verdict the evidence earns. No summary, rhetorical question, generic CTA, or forced callback.

### Format calibration

- **Short social:** one retellable claim, fast entry, compact proof, discovery-oriented rhythm.
- **Durable essay or op-ed:** mechanism, historical arc, or structural frame; depth and citation quality matter more than speed.
- **Newsletter:** smart-friend register; continuity may be used only when prior-send context is supplied.
- **Audio or podcast script:** dialogic rhythm written for speech, with room for a co-host or audience response.
- **Education or enterprise article:** accessible entry with no dumbing down; teach the commercial mechanism.

Do not assume a named channel is active or that old length constraints remain current. `content-corpus` owns current channel mandate, format, status, and editorial-versus-sales boundary.

## Correspondence

Open with purpose. Do not add a wrapper around the final draft.

Use the recipient's own authorised language where it proves listening. Drop subject pronouns where natural. Touch sentiment once, then connect it to a fact or action. State the exact low-friction next step. The email should be as short as the decision permits.

Before delivery, confirm recipient, purpose, promised attachment/link, claims, confidentiality, desired reply, and whether the task authorises drafting only. Draft approval never authorises sending.

## Editing and de-AI work

Before editing, freeze:

- claims and factual scope;
- argument and intended decision effect;
- exact quotations and attribution;
- required legal, compliance, brand, or product wording;
- terms that must remain;
- allowed change budget.

Load the full AI-tell taxonomy. Change only what fails. Preserve intentional Krish signatures. After editing, compare the locked material against the final draft and verify protected wording exactly. If a voice rule conflicts with legal meaning or evidence, truth and legal meaning win; flag the conflict rather than silently rewriting it.

## Source-material and angle options

When asked what to write from a supplied, authorised corpus, first route channel and conversion strategy correctly. Then evaluate signal:

- contradiction between what an organisation says and does;
- an unnamed structural shift;
- a tactical event revealing a strategic mechanism;
- something Krish built, observed, reversed, or watched fail;
- a claim that a smart editor could reject for an interesting reason.

For each approved option, provide the one-sentence claim, target format, fair antagonist, source artifact, audience, commercial/domain owner, reason it will land, strongest editorial objection, and the evidence needed to answer it. Do not default to the most timely obvious angle.

## Data, numbers, and quotations

Research before using a number. Distinguish current primary evidence from a bounded lived observation. Own approximations plainly. Never invent revenue, outcomes, dates, customer results, current fleet counts, or quotes.

Keep verbatim historical quotes exact. If a sharper line is newly drafted for Krish, label it as proposed language and obtain his approval before attributing it as something he already said.

When a fact is disputed, stale, inaccessible, or unsupported, resolve it through the domain owner or `evidence-research`, remove it, or qualify it visibly. Fluency is not evidence.

## Commercial mechanic

Final prose should support the approved business or personal-brand mechanic without turning every artifact into sales copy. The relevant domain owner supplies current commercial truth; `content-corpus` supplies the named channel mandate; `krish-content-marketer` supplies buyer strategy. This skill expresses their approved packet in Krish's voice.

The artifact does not need a CTA. It does need a known job. If the commercial mechanic is unknown and would materially change the writing, ask one high-signal question or return to `strategy-brief`.

## Long-form delivery packet

Use this wrapper for original long-form prose unless the requested format makes it intrusive:

```text
ANGLE: [approved one-sentence claim]
ANTAGONIST: [fair target, named or internal-only]
SOURCE ARTIFACT: [authorised grounding, or external-commentary limitation]
COMMERCIAL MECHANIC: [verified domain or personal-brand job]
GEAR: [A enterprise / B builder]
WHY THIS WORKS: [why the claim matters and what familiar frame it rejects]
DRAFT:
[final prose]
ALTERNATIVE ANGLE: [only when a meaningfully different approved option remains useful]
```

Correspondence receives no wrapper: provide the draft ready to inspect, while keeping send authority separate.
