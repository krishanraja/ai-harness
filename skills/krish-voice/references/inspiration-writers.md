# Inspiration writers

Version 1, 7 November 2026. Status: merged on Krish's instruction on 8 November 2026 ("Merge to main"); it governs from main.

These are the writers Krish named as voices he finds inspiring. They live in his Drive folder for that purpose (`voice` inspiration, the folder inspirationhouse reads as lane `voice`). This file holds **the moves they make, never their wording**. A move here is a technique Krish may borrow inside his own voice. Where a move conflicts with `voice-doctrine.md`, `voice-profile.md` or his rulings, the doctrine wins and the move is listed under "refuse".

Read it when drafting a LinkedIn post, a short social post or an email in his name, after the doctrine and his register in `voice-profile.md`. It sharpens his voice; it never replaces it. Do not quote these writers' lines, imitate their slang, or borrow their biography. A draft that reads like Alex Lieberman or Tyler Denk has failed. A draft that reads like Krish on a very good day has passed.

## How this file grows

- A new screenshot in the folder becomes evidence in inspirationhouse and, on Krish's yes, an approved voice rule (`approved-taste.md`). That path is unchanged.
- A new writer, or a new move for an existing writer, is added here only by a pull request Krish merges, with the posts that show it. Evidence comes from his saved screenshots and from Control Center's weekly post read (`content_creators`); that read, and any spend it makes, is governed in Control Center under his approvals there, never by this file, and it never edits this file.
- Every move cites at least two of the writer's own public posts, unless marked "one post".

## The shared moves

All three writers do these. Most already sit in Krish's own Core (`voice-profile.md`), which is why they suit him.

1. **The first line is the whole claim.** It stands alone and makes a complete point, with no setup before it. (Krish Core 1.)
2. **A specific number early.** A headcount, a dollar figure, a time span, a percentage. Only a number Krish can source for the task.
3. **Proof from the front row.** "My team", "we launched", "I run". Authority comes from what they did or saw, not from a title. (Krish Core 3.)
4. **One idea per post.** Everything else is cut.
5. **Correct a common belief.** "Unsexy phase of AI", "email has been dying since the 1990s", "you probably don't need a harness". Defensible from evidence, never contrarian for sport. (Doctrine: unfashionable when evidence warrants it.)
6. **The company is proof, not the subject.** Their business appears as the evidence or the tool, rarely in the first line.
7. **Plain words, contractions, no jargon left unexplained.**
8. **A short close:** a principle in one line or a specific question that is easy to answer.
9. **No hashtags, or very few.** None is best and three at most (`linkedin-evidence.md`, rule 9).

- **The contrarian history.** "Email has been dying since the 1990s" ([post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tyler-denk_email-has-been-dying-since-the-1990s-some-activity-7406331776329023488-TNiu)). Name the old prediction, then the evidence it was wrong.

**Refuse**
- **Profanity, slang and lowercase-for-effect** ("shit", "super dope", "low-key"). Krish's casual coinages are his own ("relatable AF" is from 2021; voice-profile Core 9) and his written register is dry (ruling 4). Never borrow another writer's slang.
- **Naming and shaming a company.** It breaks the Kind Rule when it lands on people; aim the spike at the decision or the incentive.
- **Emoji bullets on launch posts.** Retired by ruling 3: one emoji for feeling, on LinkedIn only.

## Adapting a move: one worked check

Move: Tyler's number-first opener. Krish version: a first line with a number Krish has approved, about his own work, followed by the reframe (Core 2), not by a growth brag. Check it against the doctrine: British spelling, no em dash, no banned word, no invented figure, and the close invites rather than pushes. If any check fails, the move does not ship.
