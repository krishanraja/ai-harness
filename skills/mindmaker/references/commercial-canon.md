## Identity and worldview

Mindmaker is Krish Raja's operator-led AI advisory, education, and product business. It helps senior leaders and organisations make better AI decisions and build useful operating capability. It should feel like a practitioner opening the machine, not a consultant presenting a deck.

Durable thesis:

- AI literacy precedes AI strategy.
- AI should amplify the leader's thinking and execution, not erase the leader.
- Break work into bricks, choose deliberately what AI carries, protect the work that depends on human judgment, and design the new human functions an agent-native organisation needs.
- Saving time is not the final promise. Reinvesting recovered capacity into higher-value human work is.
- The buyer may privately feel exposed or behind, but customer-facing language must not shame them or sell fear. Kindness points the spike at broken systems, hype, and weak ideas rather than at the buyer.

Mindmaker OS is admissible proof because Krish operates the approach he teaches. Use a current, verified artifact rather than a memorised agent count or performance claim.

## Buyer families

Use these as durable families, then verify the current segmentation in the private commercial repository before material work:

- **AI leaders:** senior operators accountable for AI decisions who need personal fluency and repeatable judgment.
- **Executive teams:** leadership groups that need alignment, priorities, and an operating path rather than scattered experiments.
- **AI products:** organisations commercialising an AI product or redesigning a revenue engine around AI.
- **Capital and portfolio operators:** investors or operating partners assessing and improving AI readiness across a fund or portfolio. Treat this as a framing of enterprise work when current canon does so, not automatically as a separate offer.
- **Editorial and product-led learners:** leaders who enter through Mindmaker Live or a free Maven Lightning Lesson before deciding whether CTRL or managed advisory is useful.

Never call a buyer a beginner. Reflect the accountable moment and practical gap without mocking competence. For outbound or sales copy, retrieve the current ICP document and the person's actual language.

## Proof and expression

Prefer proof in this order:

1. a current artifact Krish built, shipped, measured, or watched fail;

3. current primary research with a clearly bounded claim.

Never invent an outcome, quote, client result, guarantee, fleet count, or operating statistic. Avoid vague claims such as "practical," "hands-on," or "operator-led" unless the artifact demonstrates them.
