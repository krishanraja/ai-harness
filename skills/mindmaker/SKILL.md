## 1. What Mindmaker Is

Mindmaker is an AI advisory and product business operating as Mindmaker LLC (and trading as Mindmaker AI). It positions Krish as an operator-advisor who builds commercial engines for businesses using AI, not a consultant who delivers decks.

The thesis in one line: **AI literacy precedes AI strategy.** Most AI initiatives stall because leaders cannot make confident AI decisions, not because the technology is missing. Mindmaker exists to fix that by working with the human, not the tool stack.

The positioning in one sentence: **Most consultants help your business. Mindmaker helps you operate your business with AI.**

### 1.1 The worldview under the slogan

The slogans above are the surface. This is the belief Mindmaker actually runs on, in Krish's own framing. Use it to keep messaging anchored when the marketing words start drifting.

The premise: **a leader can increase the output of their own mind using AI.** This is amplification of the individual, not replacement of the individual. Mindmaker is not about cutting the leader out, it is about making the leader's thinking go further. Hold this distinction hard, because every lazy AI take collapses it.

Three calls decide whether a leader comes out of this decade ahead, and the whole Mindmaker curriculum exists to help them make these calls well:
1. **Which bricks AI replaces.** Decompose the work into bricks, then decide deliberately which bricks an agent takes and which it never touches. The brick language is load-bearing and recurs across the OS content (capture, process, decide, produce, distribute).
2. **What to get excellent at because AI will never touch it.** Judgment, taste, trust, the call under uncertainty. The half of the job that does not automate, and where a leader's remaining edge concentrates.
3. **Which net-new human functions to design.** The roles that do not exist yet but will, because every agent-native business will need them and almost nobody is building them.

The reinvestment dividend is the point, not the time saved. The argument is never "AI saves you time." It is "AI saves you time or raises your quality, and you reinvest that recovered capacity into the work only a human can do." Time saved is the setup. Reinvestment is the payoff. Copy that stops at "save time" has only told half the story.

**The expensed-buyer constraint (this governs how the worldview is expressed).** Underneath all of this sits a genuinely existential stake: these are the moves that stop a leader being made redundant by the shift. But the cohort is bought by a business for an employee, so the existential stakes stay in the engine and never on the surface. No survival framing, no extinction language, no fear as the hook. The belief can be existential; the expression stays professional and aspirational, because nobody expenses fear. When a name or a piece of copy starts leaning on doom, it has broken this rule.

The proof: Krish runs an autonomous AI operating system (Mindmaker OS) and uses the same playbook he sells. Uptime of the OS is the product demo.

**Agent count (canonical, do not mix).** Public-facing copy says **14 agents**, which matches every live claim on the site, Maven, and the bios. The OS in fact now runs **17 named agents**. Some legacy docs still say 13. The rule: use 14 in anything customer-facing, know the real number is 17, and never let two different counts appear in the same asset. If the public number is ever trued up, change it everywhere at once, not piecemeal.

## 3. The Three ICPs

Mindmaker organizes the commercial surface by ICP, not by product. Same product is framed differently for each buyer.

### 3.1 Individual leaders → `/cohort`
Senior leaders with a nervous AI decision to make. Title patterns: VP, SVP, Director, Head of, Chief. Budget authority: personal or sub-departmental. Pain: "I've tried the tools, watched the tutorials, but I am not faster or sharper." Primary offer: AI-Fluent Executive Cohort ($2,500). Secondary: Workshops ($599) as the entry rung.

**The buyer psychology: the Accountable Delegator.** The individual-leader buyer has a name and a full profile (see `ICP_ACCOUNTABLE_DELEGATOR.md`). In short: a senior commercial leader who has been made accountable for AI, talks the talk fluently in the room, and privately cannot do it themselves. They treat a leadership problem as a tooling problem. Their real driver is fear of exposure, the moment the gap shows in front of a board, a panel, or their own team. The engine that pulls them up the ladder is "the 12x": the finding that leaders who personally use AI get dramatically better outcomes than those who delegate it. Usable supporting stats, framed as reported: 95% of enterprise GenAI pilots deliver no P&L impact; only ~22% of execs feel prepared; ~58% never took AI training.

**The rule: apply this buyer implicitly, never explicitly.** Speak to the feeling, never name the archetype. Never call the buyer a beginner. Name the impostor feeling as accurate (do not reassure it away), and never point wit at the buyer's competence (the person already fears exactly that). This is the single thing that separates copy that lands from copy this buyer recoils from. The fuller expression of this lives in the Maven channel of the `content-corpus` skill; this entry is the cross-reference so the buyer is not lost.

### 3.2 Enterprise buyers → `/enterprise`
Companies commercializing AI products or trying to align an exec team on AI. Title patterns: C-suite, EVP, founder. Budget authority: departmental or full P&L. Pain: misalignment, side projects, vendor noise, no production wins. Primary offers: Signal Session ($15K), Revenue Architecture ($60-100K), Immersion ($12K).

### 3.3 Capital allocators → `/capital`
Operating Partners, Managing/General Partners, Principals at PE/VC, family offices, wealth allocators. Pain: the fund is not AI-native; portfolio companies are exposed. Primary offers: Signal Session ($15K, framed as fund-native), Revenue Architecture ($60-100K, framed as per-portco deployment).

**Same products, different framing.** Signal Session and Revenue Architecture appear on both `/enterprise` and `/capital`. The buyer pre-qualifies themselves by which page they land on (typically via outbound that targets the right ICP).

## 7. CTRL (the product)

CTRL is Mindmaker's product line. Code-named `mm-ctrl` in the repo. Lives at `ctrl.themindmaker.ai`. It is a working software product that runs independently of Mindmaker's service offers, with its own free tier, paid SKUs, and recurring subscription.

**Positioning:** "Think out loud. See what emerges." Build a portable AI double in 2 minutes. Hear 3 minutes of audio every morning that's actually about your world. Every AI tool you use already knows your context, goals, and thinking style.

**Tagline:** Clarity for Leaders.

### 7.1 What CTRL does

Four core capabilities, all built on a single voice-first capture loop:

1. **Memory Web.** Voice a thought (2 minutes, no typing). Facts are extracted, categorized, verified by the user, and encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). The Memory Web is the user's portable AI context.
2. **Edge (Leadership Amplifier).** Synthesizes the Memory Web and assessment data into a leadership profile. Sharpens strengths (Systemize, Teach, Lean Into). Covers weaknesses (Board Memos, Strategy Docs, Emails, Meeting Agendas, Templates, Frameworks). Includes interactive strength/weakness pills with feedback loops, intelligence gap detection, and guided resolution.
3. **Daily Briefing v2.** Three minutes of audio every morning, anchored to the user's actual priorities. Every segment proves its relevance with a specific profile fact. Bookmark to make an anchor persistent; Ban to kill a topic semantically (embeddings-based). Seven briefing types: Daily Brief, Macro Trends, Vendor Landscape, Competitive Intel, Boardroom Prep, AI Model Landscape, Custom Voice. Cold start solved via 11 pre-seeded industry starter beats.
4. **Context Export.** One-click export to any AI tool: ChatGPT (custom instructions), Claude (conversation context), Gemini (formatted context), Cursor (`.cursorrules`), Claude Code (`CLAUDE.md`), or raw markdown. Optimized for General Advisor, Meeting Prep, Decision Support, Code Review, Email Drafting, Strategic Planning.

Plus supporting capabilities:

- **Thinking Tools:** Decision Advisor, Meeting Prep, Team Brief, Stream of Consciousness
- **AI Literacy Diagnostic:** 10-minute assessment across six dimensions (Strategic Vision, Experimentation Culture, Delegation & Automation, Data & Decision Quality, Team Capability, Governance). Surfaces tensions, risk signals, organizational scenarios.
- **Missions & Progress:** Commit to action items from the diagnostic. Adaptive prompts based on momentum.
- **Agent Skill Builder (Phase 8, Edge Pro):** Voice-to-Claude-Skill pipeline. Triage gate → LLM → quality gate → ZIP.

## 10. The Boundary with Mindmaker OS

This is the most-often-confused distinction. Stating it explicitly:

| Mindmaker | Mindmaker OS |
|---|---|
| Commercial venture, sold to clients | Autonomous operating system, runs internally |
| One of 13 ventures in the OS portfolio | The OS that runs all 13 ventures |
| Has offers, pricing, ICPs, a website | Has agents, workflows, a Supabase database, n8n fleet |
| Lives at themindmaker.ai | Lives at krishraja10101.app.n8n.cloud + Supabase + VPS |
| Customer-facing | Operator-facing |
| Sells AI literacy and commercial sprints | Executes outbound, research, content, audit, deal flow |

**The OS includes Mindmaker as one of the ventures it manages.** Other ventures the OS manages: AdFixus, Meliora, Fractionl, Signal & Noise, CIRCLE, PULSE, and others. Mindmaker is the largest commercial surface inside the portfolio.

**Where does CTRL sit?** CTRL is *inside* Mindmaker (it is the Mindmaker product line), not a separate venture. The OS runs and operates CTRL the same way it runs the rest of Mindmaker: outbound, content, audit, deal flow. CTRL is customer-facing software; the OS is internal infrastructure. They are not the same thing and should not be conflated.

**The OS is the dogfood layer for Mindmaker.** The fact that Krish runs an autonomous 14-agent system is the proof point that he can advise others on building AI-native businesses. The `/operator` page on themindmaker.ai surfaces this dogfood explicitly.

Practical rule: if the work touches **offers, pricing, ICP, website copy, sales sequences, Maven, Substack, CTRL features or roadmap, or anything customer-facing**, use this skill.
