## Who it is for

Founders, CEOs and senior commercial leaders who can make a decision on their own, and whose decisions are big enough to matter. The sweet spot is PE or VC-backed media, adtech and data businesses (the mindmake North Star). It is not for someone who cannot move the decision, or who wants AI training, a certificate or a list of tasks to automate.

## The lanes, kept apart

Krish, 17 November 2026, to Boardy: "I think we need to get this really spot on and correct so we keep our lanes really clean but we achieve the outcomes in different threads."

- **Mindmake** sells the 30-day build. Its buyers are leaders.
- **Stages and partners** put Krish in front of many leaders at once. They are the main way leaders meet him.
- **Make Your Mind Up** needs an audience. It trades craft for reach: Krish makes a show with someone who has an audience. It never lends its own small audience, and it never carries a sales pitch.
- **Signal & Noise** is someone else's show, where Krish is Executive Host. It can carry people who want distribution. It never leads a pitch about Krish: "That's not my own IP. I'm just an executive host on someone else's podcast so it's not going to fill my cup." (Krish, 13 November 2026.)

Make Your Mind Up's own canon (`krishanraja/makeyourmindup` `project-documentation/01_MEDIA_KIT.md`, Ruling, Krish, 2026-10-28) means a show there carries his face and voice but never his name in a title, credit or description, and it shows no visible link to Mindmake beyond CTRL beta access.
