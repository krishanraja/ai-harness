# CTRL product context

## Product boundary

CTRL is a customer-facing Mindmake software product. It is not mind/make OS and not a separate internal venture. mind/make OS may operate or dogfood parts of the business, but its internal fleet, automations, and state are not CTRL features.

Use this reference for product positioning and routing. Use the current private `krishanraja/mm-ctrl` repository, its `project-documentation/`, the live product, and the relevant transaction surface for implementation facts and current claims.

## Durable product promise

CTRL helps a leader capture context, make that context portable, and receive thinking support grounded in their own world. Its durable product territory includes:

- voice-first or low-friction context capture;
- a user-controlled memory/context layer;
- leadership amplification and thinking support;
- briefings or outputs tied to the user's real priorities;
- export or reuse of context across AI tools;
- diagnostic or guided decision-support experiences.

Do not infer that every historical feature, tier, integration, assessment dimension, AI model, encryption implementation, export target, or subscription is currently available. Verify it in the current product and repository before stating it.

## Commercial role

CTRL can serve three non-exclusive roles:

1. a standalone product-led entry into Mindmake;
2. a supporting experience inside a service or education offer when currently bundled;
3. operating proof that Mindmake's method is embodied in software.

Do not reduce CTRL to a service perk or insert it into every service ladder automatically. Verify current bundling, eligibility, and transaction terms live.

## Privacy and authority

User memory, recordings, profiles, exports, billing, and authentication are sensitive. Do not expose private content, inspect browser/session storage, copy credential values, weaken access controls, or use personal data outside the task's authority.

Product changes route to `krish-design` or `krish-build`; commercial copy routes through `krish-content-marketer` and `krish-voice`; all changes require `verification-loop`. Deployment, billing edits, publication, account changes, and destructive data operations require exact action-time approval.
