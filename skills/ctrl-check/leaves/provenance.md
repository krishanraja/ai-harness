# Personal-rule provenance

Run this pass for a working profile, personal SKILL.md, custom instructions, context file, or other artifact that asserts how a named person judges or works. Resolve against the exact Compile/Build manifests before reading old deployed copies.

## What needs a person pointer

Every subject-specific directive, preference, voice rule, hard constraint, gotcha, exemplar attribution, and statement of personal judgment must resolve to an allowed current `[C…]` criterion or `[E…]` evidence id.

Pure file syntax, safety policy, platform mechanics, and governance rules use their separate contract source. Do not falsely attribute them to the person or fail them merely because they lack a person pointer.

Factual claims, dates, names, and figures use their appropriate source citation. Verify current/high-stakes truth separately; a person pointer does not make a fact true.

## Resolve each personal claim

For every personal assertion:

1. Record its exact text and locator.
2. Find the pointer and confirm the id exists in the manifest allowlist.
3. Confirm the record is current, not deleted, untested-as-active, superseded, withdrawn, or quarantined.
4. Compare the artifact's surface/situation with the source. A situated source cannot support a standing rule.
5. For text presented as the person's own, confirm known authorship, authorised audience, and verbatim equality or an explicitly approved redacted derivative.
6. Confirm all source AWAITING/untested/deleted/withdrawn exclusions survived rather than becoming confident content.

An unsupported or stale personal claim is `breaks`: delete it from the proposed package or render `NOT ESTABLISHED: <exact gap>`. Never keep it with a hedge or replace it from memory.

## Situated failure example

Evidence that rejects a deck for weekly progress updates cannot support `Never produce decks`. The corrected form must carry the source situation, such as `For weekly progress updates on this engagement ... [E12]`. For any unfamiliar deck context, the package must use its fallback/NOT ESTABLISHED path.

## Report

```text
PROVENANCE
Artifact/manifest versions and hashes: [...]
Personal assertions checked: [n]
Resolved current: [n]
Unresolved/stale/withdrawn: [exact locator, pointer, source state]
Situated overreach: [artifact text, source situation, bounded proposed form]
Verbatim/authorship failures: [exact locator and safe evidence]
AWAITING/untested/deleted/withdrawn source ids: [n]
Preserved in artifact: [n]
Governance/platform lines checked separately: [source or unresolved]
```

Do not expose private source text merely to prove the failure. Use ids, hashes, safe locators, and the minimum excerpt authorised for the review audience.
