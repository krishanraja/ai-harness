# Insight format

An insight is a true, specific tension in the audience's life that the venture can resolve. It is not a fact about the product and not a wish of the venture.

## The form

> [audience] wants [X] but [Y] because [Z]

- **audience**: a group narrow enough to picture one person in it.
- **wants**: what they are trying to get, in their terms.
- **but**: what stops them, or what they settle for instead.
- **because**: the reason the obstacle exists. This is the part most drafts leave out, and the part ideas grow from.

Each line carries either a `source` and the date it was read (`read_at`), or `assumption: true`. A line marked as an assumption may still be chosen, but the sheet says so and the first test of the idea is whether the assumption holds.

## Where insights come from

Read four points before writing a line, in any order:

1. **The audience's own words**: comments, replies, reviews, search phrasing, messages the venture has received. Counts and themes only; never a person's details.
2. **The job to be done**: the progress the buyer is trying to make when they reach for this kind of product, and what they would hire instead.
3. **The category's habit**: what every competitor says and shows. The insight usually sits where the category is silent.
4. **The venture's own record**: what has gone live, how it did, and Krish's verdicts. A tension already used on a live post is a retread unless the new idea takes it somewhere the old one did not.

## The tension test

Write one to three lines, then pick one. A line passes only when:

- the `but` and `because` are not the same clause said twice;
- swapping in another audience breaks it;
- it does not name the product (the product is the answer, not the insight);
- someone in the audience would say "that's me" without being told why.

## Stall diagnosis

Before any insight work, name what is stuck, with evidence:

| Diagnosis | Means | The desk |
|---|---|---|
| `idea` | the posts or invites reach people and do not move them | runs |
| `measurement` | nobody can read the number | runs, and every idea on the sheet is a measurement idea |
| `distribution` | the ideas never reach enough people | stops; the engine's distribution work owns it |
| `offer` | people respond but the offer does not convert | stops; the offer owner owns it |
| `attribution` | results happen but cannot be traced to the work | stops; fix the tracking first |
