## Block 2: brain write-back gap (claude-ai-chat, perplexity-computer, n8n-agents, routines)

LAYER: 3, a routing/check-scoping fix, proposed as a question rather than a bounded delta.

EVIDENCE: The same four surfaces are flagged unchanged across all three audits, e.g.
`audit:2026-09-24:claude-ai-chat`: "claude-ai-chat reads from the brain and writes nothing
back." `scripts/audit-harness.mjs:506` states the underlying norm: "Every surface should
both read from the brain and write back to it." `brain/stores.yaml` names one working
write pump, `openclaw-vps-host-state` (lines 222-227), and describes `client-native-memory`
(329-336) as deliberately one-directional by design ("scratch and never authority...
anything durable found there is proposed to the brain").

ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS: The check may be over-broad. `claude-ai-chat` and `perplexity-
computer` are hosted consumer chat surfaces with no documented API for pushing structured
writes into Supabase; requiring write-back from them may not be achievable the way it is
for `n8n-agents` or `routines`, which are OS-controlled and could plausibly get a pump
built. Recurrence here may be measuring an unfixable property rather than a defect, which
is exactly the case `ctrl-capture`'s "non-firing means nothing without opportunities"
guards against in reverse: three audits of the same unchanged finding without any
remediation attempt is weak evidence of urgency, not strong evidence of a fixable gap.

IF WRONG: If this is left as a blanket check, it will keep flagging `claude-ai-chat` and
`perplexity-computer` indefinitely with no remediation path, training the audit reader to
discount the whole finding class, including the two surfaces (`n8n-agents`, `routines`)
where a write-back pump is plausible and worth building.

VALIDATION: The proposal is a question, not a delta, per `ctrl-capture`'s "a proposal may
ask a question without supplying a delta when evidence is insufficient." The question for
the owner: should `brain/stores.yaml` gain a per-surface `write_back_capable` field (with
a stated reason) so the audit can distinguish "not yet built" from "not architecturally
possible," splitting one noisy four-way finding into two smaller, actionable ones. If
accepted, the eventual regression case is: `n8n-agents`/`routines` still flag until a pump
exists; `claude-ai-chat`/`perplexity-computer` stop flagging once marked incapable with a
recorded reason.

PRIOR KNOWN-GOOD + ROLLBACK: No file changes here. If the owner accepts the question and a
future change adds the field, rollback is a straight revert of that field addition to
`brain/stores.yaml`'s current schema.

ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS: The brief's framing ("exclusions sit at the end") is imprecise;
measured position is closer to the middle than the end for two of the three skills. That

still risks cutting content after that clause, wherever it sits, and all three
descriptions carry material after their exclusion language. The imprecision is in the
brief's restatement, not in the recurring three-week signal itself.

IF WRONG: If descriptions are shortened aggressively to "fix" this, Gate 2's requirement
that "description includes material exclusions and collision precedence where ambiguity
is plausible" could be lost for the sake of length, trading a truncation risk for a
certainty of missing exclusion language on every client, which is worse.

VALIDATION: Per Gate 2's held-out trigger suite (positive/negative/adversarial cases per
skill class), the deterministic check is: on the shortest supported client context budget,
does the truncated description still retain the exclusion/collision clause. Propose to the
owner that `contract/active-skill-quality-standard.md` Gate 2 gain an explicit rule that
material exclusions must survive a stated truncation budget (front-loaded or restated
compactly), and that these three skills' descriptions be re-drafted against that budget
before their next `reviewed` date. This is a proposal only; Gate 2 and the three skill
files are unedited here.

PRIOR KNOWN-GOOD + ROLLBACK: Current descriptions (989/941/939 characters, as measured
above) are the prior known-good. Any future edit should record the prior character count
and clause position so a regression can be measured, and revert to the current text if
the new draft fails collision or trigger-precision tests.
