## What changed and why

The strategist could only be reached on its own schedule (the Sunday board and the U5 watch). A session rebuilding a site or a value proposition had no route to it, so it fell back on whatever framework the model remembered. krishanraja/strategist#23 gives it a front door: `src/cli.ts route "<job>"` sizes any job and names its own-words methods (positioning, value proposition, offer and pricing, buyer evidence, page review, unit economics, pre-mortem, the site and value proposition rebuild), and `qualify check` grades every MVP card before it gets a week of build.

This change records that route where sessions look:

- `state/fleet.yaml`: the strategist row's capability, call and when now name the on-demand route; `references/capabilities.md` is rendered from it.
- `adapters/cloud/account-routing.md`: one line under "Use the narrowest owner" sends any strategy job to the strategist, and keeps the producers as the writers and designers.
- `scripts/test-account-sync.mjs`: a case holds the line.

## Walls

Nothing widens. The strategist proposes; it never writes a venture's final words or designs, and never ships, sends, posts or spends.
