# The default skills name the strategist, 2026-11-28

Status: proposed. Krish asked for it on 2026-11-28.

## The ruling

Krish, 2026-11-28: "just making sure that krish-principles, strategy-brief, and krish-design and all the other skills are integrated with this and fully up to date? That is what new sessions use by default".

## What changed and why

After krishanraja/ai-harness#331 and #332 a session reached the strategist only through `references/capabilities.md`, the cloud routing line and the routing contract. The skills a new session loads by default did not name it, so a session following krish-principles, strategy-brief or krish-design alone would never hand a strategy argument to it. One routing line or row is added to each, at the place that skill already routes work:

- `krish-principles`, "Route ownership precisely": a venture's strategy argument and its test go to `krishanraja/strategist` through its router; principles frames, the strategist builds the argument and the test, the producers write and design.
- `strategy-brief`, "Routing rules for common collisions": a site or value proposition rebuild runs the strategist's playbook first; strategy-brief still plans the session.
- `krish-design`, "Routing and handoffs": what a page or brand must argue, and whether a change earns a test, go to the strategist before design; design keeps taste and interaction.
- `krish-content-marketer`, "Routing and collisions": the strategy argument upstream of conversion.
- `mindmake`, "Routing and collisions": the strategist reads Mindmake's facts and never restates them.

The strategist's own capabilities, generated from its code, are in krishanraja/strategist `docs/CAPABILITIES.md` (strategist#27, merged 2026-11-28).

## Walls

Nothing widens. The strategist proposes; it never writes final words or designs, and never sends, posts or spends. The narrowest-skill rule and the boundary recorded in the routing contract (`collision-rules.krishanraja-strategist-builds-a`) stand.

## Which part of the North Star it moves

The corrections Krish gives twice in a week: "you did not know that existed", for the strategist, at the skills every session loads first.
