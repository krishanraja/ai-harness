## The source prompts

Seven prompts from a carousel by the Instagram account getintoai ("Want to grow your Instagram faster? These 7 Ch..."). Krish saved them as screenshots to his Drive on 2026-10-30, and they were read that day. They are quoted here as the record. `contract/channel-tasks.md` supersedes their wording: they are not operating instructions.

1. Instagram algorithm research: "Research the latest Instagram ranking guidance, feature updates, and relevant content trends using current sources. Explain which signals and formats matter for my account, separate official guidance from speculation, and give me 5 practical actions to test this week. Never present outdated algorithm theories as current facts"
2. Complete profile audit: "Analyze my username, profile photo, name field, bio, niche clarity, pinned posts, content quality, and call to action ... Identify what could stop a visitor from following me, then give me exact changes to improve clarity, credibility, discoverability, and conversions"
3. Content ideas generator: "Generate 30 content ideas based on their biggest problems, desires, questions, mistakes, and misconceptions. For each idea, provide a strong angle, opening hook, recommended format, core value, and reason someone might save or share it."
4. Reels that hold attention: "Write 5 hook options, select the strongest, and create a complete script with opening visuals, on-screen text, scene-by-scene pacing, useful payoff, and a natural CTA ... Optimize for viewer satisfaction and retention, not clickbait."
5. Captions that drive action: "Write 5 Instagram captions ... Test different approaches, such as storytelling, practical advice, a strong opinion, a relatable problem, and a step-by-step solution."
6. The latest trends: "Give me 10 opportunities ranked by relevance, originality, audience demand, and ease of execution. For each, explain how to adapt it to my niche without copying another creator. Include dates and sources, and never invent trend data."
7. The 30-day growth plan: "Build a realistic 30-day Instagram growth plan with weekly themes, reels, carousels, stories, collaborations, community engagement, and analytics reviews ... Adapt the plan to my capacity rather than recommending an unsustainable posting schedule."

## What changed from the prompts, and why

- **A channel is never the goal.** No engine's North Star counts followers, so every output names the signal it serves.
- **The prompts' counts are starting values.** An engine changes one when its measured results or its capacity say so.
- **Ownership of the words stays with existing owners.** Hooks and captions are alternatives; `krish-content-marketer` and `krish-voice` keep angle and final prose.
- **Engines own what each task accomplishes.** This follows the correction above.
