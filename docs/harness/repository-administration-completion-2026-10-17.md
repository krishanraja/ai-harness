## Autonomous operation and recovery

Intake completion and relevant accepted-source pushes trigger reconciliation.
Nightly catch-up runs at 22:10 UTC; scoped proposal interpretation runs Sunday
at 23:10 UTC. New inferred meaning uses the existing limited owner-review
queue. Already accepted views are checked, merged and read back automatically.
Reporting cannot trigger a publication loop. `mindmake` remains excluded.

The [operations guide](repository-administration.md),
[measurement handoff](repository-measurements.md) and
[deployment ledger](../../state/repository-administration/deployments.jsonl)
name the owners, proof requirements, prior-known-good bases and exact rollback
procedure. Rollback preserves unambiguous later human edits, refuses drift and
retains immutable history. Both pilot rollback planners were exercised; no
application action or rollback was performed during this completion audit.
The current-source replay uses the actual deployment bytes as its rollback
base and preserves content-engine's 18 later nonblank human instruction lines,
as well as a synthetic appended note. It refuses edits to a newly owned file.

No further implementation or rollout step remains in the approved plan.
Future source receipts and real work will update the measurements without
turning missing evidence into a success claim.
