## 4. Governor facts

F63. Path and cadence: `/root/.openclaw/workspace/scripts/n8n-exec-governor.py`, hourly, deliberately outside n8n's own budget so it cannot be starved as the cap approaches. ARCH:520.

F64. Mechanism as designed: a cumulative per-cycle execution counter, robust to n8n's history-retention pruning. Warns at 7,000 per cycle, or a projection of 9,500 or more, into `/var/log/os-pull-only-alerts.log` (it warned the ops Telegram until 2026-09-06). Trips at 8,000 per cycle by deactivating every active workflow not in a critical whitelist: Stripe, Approval, Error-Monitor, Orchestrator, Control-Center-Sync, Status-Receiver, Critical-Infra-Monitor. Re-arms on cycle rollover. ARCH:520.

F65. The n8n Cloud plan cap is 10,000 executions a month, confirmed 2026-07-01; the governance goal is to stay at or under 8,000 with margin so critical workflows are never starved near month end. ARCH:511.
