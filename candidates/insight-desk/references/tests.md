# The screen

Every idea on the sheet faces every test. Each result is `pass`, `fail` or `unavailable`, with a one-sentence reason that names the evidence. No test produces a number, and no 1 to 10 score, rank or weighted total reaches Krish. An idea leads only when every test passed.

## Before the screen: the warm-up discard

Write the three ideas anyone in the category would reach for first, before any method runs. Record them as `discarded_obvious` and send them to divergent `score` as the obvious set. An idea on the sheet that matches one of them, word for word or as a close paraphrase, fails the screen.

- **Pass evidence:** three discarded ideas written down before divergence, and no sheet idea that restates one.
- **Fail:** fewer than three, written after the ideas, or a sheet idea that is one of them with new wording.

## Specificity swap (`specificity`)

Replace the venture's name with a named competitor or a neighbouring brand and read the idea again.

- **Pass:** the idea breaks: it no longer makes sense, or makes a claim the competitor cannot make. Name the competitor used and what broke.
- **Fail:** it still works for the competitor. A generic idea is a template.

## Share (`share`)

For a consumer venture: would a friend send it to one named kind of friend? For a business venture: would a peer forward it to a colleague with one line of their own?

- **Pass:** state who sends it, to whom, and the line they would add.
- **Fail:** the only reason to share is the brand asking.

## Analogue (`analogue`)

Find one real piece of work, in any field, that used the same mechanic and worked. Link it and read it today.

- **Pass:** `url` and `read_at` recorded, with `read_at` the day the sheet is made (the date in `made_at`), and the reason names what the analogue proves.
- **Unavailable:** no analogue found. The idea may stay on the sheet but cannot lead.
- **Fail:** the analogue is invented, unread, or uses a different mechanic.

## Floor and claims (`floor`)

Read the venture's own floor, copy doctrine and claims gate, as named in its adapter.

- **Pass:** name the files read and the rule closest to being tripped.
- **Fail:** the idea needs a claim, product image, person or joke the venture's rules forbid.
- **Unavailable:** a rule file could not be read. The idea cannot lead.

## Kill the favourite (`kill_the_favourite`)

Write the strongest honest attack on the lead idea, as its harshest fair critic would. Divergent `challenge` may be called at most once per sheet for this.

- **Pass:** the attack is written and the idea survives it, with the reason.
- **Fail:** the attack lands and the idea cannot answer it. Pick the next idea and attack that one.

## Conversion path

Not a test result field but a gate: every idea names the offer, the tracked link and why a viewer acts now. An idea without all three is held off the sheet.

## Idea level

Every idea names its level, so Krish sees what kind of commitment he is approving:

| Level | Means |
|---|---|
| `execution` | one piece of work |
| `campaign` | a short series around one claim |
| `platform` | a repeatable format the engine can run for weeks |
| `business` | a change to the offer, the product or how it is sold |
| `measurement` | work whose purpose is to make the number readable |

## No scores

The sheet carries results and reasons only. A number in a test field, a `score` key anywhere, or a ranking presented as a total fails validation. So does a score or ranking written in words in any field, such as "9/10", "scored 8", "ranked first" or "40 points".
