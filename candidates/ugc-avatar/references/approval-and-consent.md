# Approval and consent for AI people

Status: approved

Krish ruled on 2026-11-15, in his words: "happy to use everywhere with my explicit approval before deciding to use it, and then my explicit approval before it goes out to ensure 10/10 quality. No need for AI labels anywhere, at any point." It is recorded as `Ruling (Krish, 2026-11-15): AI people may be used anywhere, with his explicit yes before one is used and again on the finished asset before it goes out; no AI labels anywhere.` It replaces the draft disclosure and consent rule, which he never approved. Only Krish changes this rule, and the commit that changes it carries his ruling.

## Two yeses

Any venture may use an AI person. Each use needs two explicit yeses from Krish, each recorded with his own words:

1. **Before it is used.** He says yes to an AI person in one named piece, before anything is rolled, quoted or generated for it. A yes covers that one piece and never carries over to another.
2. **Before it goes out.** He says yes to the finished file, named by its SHA-256, after the independent judge has passed it and only when it meets his 10/10 bar. Any edit after his yes makes a new file, and a new file needs a new yes.

`scripts/avatar.py` refuses a roll or a paid step without the first yes, and refuses the release without both.

## No AI label

No AI label goes anywhere, at any point: not on screen, not in the caption, not in the voice, and not switched on in a platform's settings. The roller refuses a script that carries one.

## What stays, and why

- **Never a customer, buyer, reviewer or testimonial.** US rules ban fake reviews and testimonials, AI-generated ones included, so an AI person never says or suggests they bought, used, unboxed, rated or reviewed anything.
- **Never a real person's likeness without their recorded consent.** A face, a voice and a name belong to the person, so using one needs their written consent naming the venture, the use, how long it lasts and how to withdraw; it is kept with the venture's private records, never in a public repository, and the script cites its id as `consent_id`. A persona's voice is designed from a written description unless that consent covers a real voice, and a withdrawal goes to Krish to take the posts down the same day.
- **Adults only, 18 or over.** No AI person may look like a child, so the roller refuses any age bound under 18 and any words that describe one, and never raises a low bound quietly.
- **No generation without a credit reservation and his yes on the quote.** Every generation spends money, and spending needs Krish's yes on the exact quote, with the credits reserved, immediately before it.
- **No borrowed authority.** An AI person never plays a doctor, a vet, a lawyer or any credentialed expert giving advice, because a viewer would trust advice no qualified person gave.
