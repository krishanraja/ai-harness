# Disclosure and consent for AI people

Status: draft

This is a draft for Krish to approve, change or reject before anyone uses an AI person. While the line above says draft, `scripts/avatar.py gate` refuses every generation. Only Krish's approval changes it to approved, recorded with his ruling in the commit that changes it.

## The rule as drafted

1. **An AI person is labelled as AI wherever they are shown.** The label is plain, in the words below, and it is never hidden, cropped, shrunk, muted or switched off later. Where it appears is the choice below.
2. **Never a real customer, buyer or testimonial.** An AI person never says or suggests they bought, received, used, unboxed, rated or reviewed the product, and never stands in for one who did. No star ratings, no "my order", no before and after, no "verified".
3. **Never a real person's likeness without their recorded consent.** That covers a face, a voice and a name. Consent is written, by that person, and names the venture, the use, how long it lasts and how they withdraw it. It is kept where the venture keeps private records, never in a public repository, and the look record cites its id. Until this rule is approved, no real likeness is used at all.
4. **Adults only.** Every AI person is 18 or over and looks it. The roller refuses any age bound under 18 and any words that describe a child.
5. **No borrowed authority.** An AI person never plays a doctor, a vet, a lawyer or any credentialed expert giving advice.
6. **Designed voices only.** A persona's voice is designed from a written description, never cloned from a real person's recording without consent under rule 3.
7. **Withdrawal is honoured.** If someone withdraws consent, every post using their likeness is put to Krish to take down, the same day.

## The label

Proposed words: **"This person is AI-generated."**

Where it shows (Krish to choose):

- **A.** The platform's own AI label switched on, and the label line first in the caption. Nothing on the picture itself.
- **B.** As A, and the label on screen for the first three seconds the person is shown.
- **C.** As B, and the label on screen for as long as the person is shown.

Recommended: **B**. The platform label and the caption line can be missed by someone who watches with the caption folded away; three seconds on screen is seen by everyone who sees the face, and it leaves the rest of the frame clean.

Heartside's own floor bars badges and labels on its posts (`no-badges`) and keeps the platforms' AI label off for its dog reels (`no-ai-label`). Neither was written for AI people, and this rule covers people only, never dogs. If Krish approves both this rule and an AI person for Heartside, the label here applies to that post alone; the dog rules stay as they are.

## What approval looks like

Krish says yes to the rule, with or without changes, and names the label words and where the label shows. The commit that sets `Status: approved` carries his ruling line and his exact words.
