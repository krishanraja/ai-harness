# Stack contracts

## Onboarding
Target: first meaningful value with minimum justified friction. Inspect first-value completion, time to value, signup abandonment, permissions, and day-one return. Guard against premature data collection and inaccessible setup.

## Activation
Target: a small act of ownership after value. Derive the activation action from retained cohorts. Guard against mistaking signup or session count for commitment.

## Retention
Target: recurring core value after novelty fades. Inspect cohorts and repeated core actions. Guard against message volume, forced streaks, and artificial lock-in.

## Monetisation
Target: informed payment for understood value. Inspect qualified paywall exposure, conversion, paid retention, refund, cancellation, and support contacts. Guard against obscured price, renewal, limits, cancellation, and false urgency.

## Engagement
Target: valuable depth in a responsive session. Inspect core-action depth, discovery, and completion. Guard against raw-time optimisation and interaction clutter.

## Habit formation
Target: a self-directed routine around recurring value. Inspect self-initiated returns and notification dependence. Guard against guilt, compulsion, punitive resets, and loss framing.

## Growth and viral
Target: qualified exposure that benefits sender and recipient. Inspect share creation, invite acceptance, recipient activation, spam reports, and privacy complaints.

## Conversion optimisation
Target: completion of an already-wanted action. Inspect step abandonment and downstream regret, cancellation, disputes, or reversals. Guard against coercive defaults and manufactured urgency.

## Trust building
Target: confident, informed, recoverable action. Inspect completion, recovery success, disputes, support contacts, and permission revocation. Guard against vague status and irreversible surprises.

## Experience refinement
Target: coherent quality in waiting, errors, emptiness, progress, and achievement. Inspect task recovery, defects, accessibility, and perceived quality. Guard against ornamental motion and disproportionate celebration.

## Intent shaping
Target: visible adaptation to user goals. Inspect usefulness, goal completion, overrides, dismissals, and corrections. Guard against hidden steering and opaque profiling.

## Premium positioning
Target: paid value supported by real craft and differentiation. Inspect premium adoption and retention. Guard against fake processing, gratuitous friction, discount dependence, and hollow exclusivity.
