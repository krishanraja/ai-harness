# The stage check

Most revenue method assumes a company with buyers, a sales team and years of history. None of that is true of an early venture, and running it anyway produces confident numbers about nothing. The stage check decides what may run at all.

## The verdict

| What the readings show | Verdict | What the sheet carries |
|---|---|---|
| No fresh counted step, or no buyer seen in the window | `measure-first` | the reading plan only: which count, from where, by when, owned by whom |
| At least one fresh counted step and at least one buyer seen | `run` | steps 4 to 7 of the loop, limited by the table below |

A buyer is someone who did something only a buyer does: paid, booked, replied with a date, renewed. Interest, a view or a like is not a buyer action, and a stage moves only on a buyer action.

`measure-first` is not a failure. It is the honest output for a venture nobody is reading yet, and it stops the desk from inventing a constraint.

## What runs at each stage

The adapter names its business shape. The desk runs only the rules for that shape.

| Shape | Runs | Does not run |
|---|---|---|
| Outbound service (meetings, proofs, retainers) | the throughput check; the stall rule on rows; buyer-action gates between steps; a dated qualify-or-close verdict on each row past its window; revival only for people who engaged | cohort retention; price tests before any buyer has paid |
| Consumer store (posts, sessions, carts, orders) | the funnel by source and by gift; value per order; the offer-terms check on any purchase copy | anything that needs a named buyer; discount tests without a second gift to trade against |
| Subscription publication (posts, visits, free and paid readers, renewals) | gross against net retention by cohort; the cohort leak split; discount only as the last lever, and always traded for something | per-row stall rules |
| Held engine | the adapter's declared path and entry gate, kept local | any ask on Krish's list, until he releases the engine |
| Unmeasured | the reading plan | everything else |

## The stall rule

A row (a person, a deal, a draft) is stalled when it has sat at one step past the window the adapter's history sets, with no buyer action. Thresholds come from that engine's own history, are labelled assumptions, and bind only after Krish's yes. A stalled row gets one dated verdict: move it on with a named next step, or close it. It never sits open without a date.

## Revival

A person who went quiet is revived only if they engaged first (replied, met, bought). Revival is at most one catch-up, written as a person writes one, inside the engine's own outreach rulings. A person who never engaged is not a revival.

## Trust band by stage

The thinner the history, the wider the band. A rate resting on fewer than about twenty cases gets a wide band, written as such; a rate resting on one week is labelled an assumption, not a rate.
